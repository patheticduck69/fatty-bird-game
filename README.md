## 🤔 What is this?

You remember Flappy Bird. One bird. One button. Pipes. Rage.

Fatty Bird keeps the rage and fixes the "one bird" problem. It's a free browser game with more characters, more skills, more ways to die, and a cave. Yes, a cave. With a dragon. You'll see.

Open the link, tap, and you're playing in seconds. No download, no account, no ads.

## ✨ Why you'll stay "just one more run"

- 🐦 **Six characters.** Each one is fat in its own special way, and each has its own skill.
- ⚡ **Skills and power-ups.** Pulse, shield, teleport and more. Use them wisely, or don't. We're not your mom.
- 🏔️ **Caves.** Every so often the pipes politely leave and something much bigger shows up.
- 💣 **Surprises.** The sky has opinions. Some of them explode.
- 🪙 **Coins and a store.** Collect coins, spend coins, regret spending coins.
- 🏆 **Online leaderboards.** Daily, weekly and all-time. Find out exactly how much better your friend is than you.
- 🎵 **Music and animated backgrounds.** Because falling should look good too.
- 📱 **Built for phones.** One thumb is all you need. The bird will still betray you.

> *We're deliberately not listing every secret here. Part of the fun is finding out what happens at score 30. (Spoiler: the bird has no sympathy.)*

## 🎮 How to play

| You do this | Fatty Bird does this |
|---|---|
| **Tap / click / Space** | Flap |
| **E** | Character skill |
| **Q / W / S** | Pulse / Blink / Shield |
| **Pause button** | Takes a breath for you |

Don't touch the pipes. Don't touch the ground. Everything else is a suggestion.
